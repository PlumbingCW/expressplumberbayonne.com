Main line backed up: the first minutes.

Sewer line cleaning clears the pipe between your house and the street connection. A heavy-duty cable machine with cutting heads opens the clog, and jetting washes the walls. Most of the time the plumber works through the cleanout, an access pipe with a cap in the basement floor or at the foundation.

Call the emergency line when sewage is backing into a floor drain or tub, when toilets will not flush, or when drains in several rooms slow at the same time. Stop using water immediately, because everything you flush goes to the same blocked pipe.

Cabling, jetting and a camera for the main line.

The plumber chooses the tool from what the line allows. A cable machine breaks through roots and solid blockages. Jetting scours grease and scale from the pipe wall. Main lines in Bayonne: who handles what.

Veolia operates the water mains and sewer lines in Bayonne, and the city keeps ownership. Your line from the building to the street connection is generally your responsibility, and the street main belongs to the system. If neighbors on your block are backing up at the same time, call Veolia first.

Heavy rain adds a different cause. The city has posted notices for a 36-inch relief sewer and an underground basin near Cottage Street Park, aimed at flooding. If your backup follows a storm and the line scopes clear, tell Veolia, because the cause may be capacity in the street.

A shop or restaurant on a commercial street such as Broadway can share a building drain with apartments above it. A grease clog in a shared line shows up in the wrong unit. Ask the owner before opening a shared cleanout, and call us if it is locked or buried.
