Signs the tank has failed and needs replacing.

A water heater is a steel tank with a glass-like lining and a sacrificial anode rod that corrodes first to protect it. Once the rod is gone and the lining cracks, the steel rusts through and the tank leaks. That failure cannot be repaired, so the plumber replaces the unit.

Water from the tank body.

Not from a fitting. Seeping rust marks or a puddle that returns after you wipe it up.

Rusty hot water.

Rust-colored water from hot taps only, with clear cold water, points to the tank.

Repeated repairs.

When a second part fails in a short time, a new heater often costs less than more repairs.

Age and sediment.

Older tanks lose capacity and rumble as sediment hardens on the bottom.

If the problem might be a part, ask for a water heater repair diagnosis first. A plumber should tell you honestly which path costs less over the next few years.

A replacement heater falls under the New Jersey plumbing subcode, which adopts a published standard, the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated/2021, with state changes. Ask the Bayonne building department whether your swap needs a permit and an inspection.

Emergency replacement: what happens when the tank is leaking.

In an emergency the plumber shuts off the water and fuel, drains what is left in the tank and removes it. Then the new tank is set, connected to supply, relief valve and venting, and tested. Many replacements finish in one visit when the right size and fuel type are on the truck.

Do not wait out a leaking tank. A slow leak can turn into a full release, and a finished basement floor, a washer or a furnace nearby can all be damaged. Close the cold valve above the unit and call the 24/7 line.

Replacement in Bayonne basements and mixed-use buildings.

Heaters often sit in basements shared with a boiler, laundry and floor drain, or in a utility closet in a two-family house. Access, venting path and gas line routing change the work. The plumber looks at those before quoting the job.

The city of Bayonne building department sits at 630 Avenue C. Permit rules vary by project, so ask that office what applies to a replacement in your building. The plumber can tell you what is typically filed.

After the January 2026 boil water advisory, the city suggested that heaters set below 113 degrees Fahrenheit be drained and refilled. If your old tank has sat for years and was never flushed, that is one more reason to consider replacement before the next failure.

Insurance note.

A tank that fails and floods a room may be covered as sudden water damage. Replacement of the tank itself is usually not covered. Photograph the unit, the label and the damage before cleanup.
