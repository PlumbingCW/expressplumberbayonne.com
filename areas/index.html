Where Bayonne Express Plumbing works.

Bayonne is a peninsula between Newark Bay and the Kill Van Kull, and it has one ZIP code, 07002. Most calls come from there. Jersey City is the only land neighbor, and Hoboken lies farther up the Hudson waterfront.

Choose a town below for ZIP codes, the water and sewer notes that matter there and the services callers ask for most.

Jersey City, NJ.

Jersey City is Bayonne's only land neighbor. One 24/7 line reaches ZIP codes 07305, 07304, 07302 and 07306.

Hoboken, NJ.

Hoboken is reached through Jersey City. One 24/7 line covers the whole city, ZIP code 07030.

Bayonne neighborhoods we dispatch to every day.

Bergen Point sits at the southern tip, at the foot of the Bayonne Bridge and across the Kill Van Kull from Staten Island. Constable Hook and Port Johnson hold the industrial and waterfront blocks to the east and north. Midtown Bayonne runs along the Broadway corridor where our office is.

All of Bayonne shares ZIP code 07002, so a ZIP does not tell a dispatcher which end of the city you are on. Give the street and the nearest cross street when you call (718) 412-8453, and mention a basement unit or a rear entrance.

Jersey City and Hoboken.

Jersey City borders Bayonne, and a Jersey City emergency plumber page lists the ZIP codes we serve there, from 07305 along the Bayonne line to 07302 downtown. Neighborhoods such as Greenville fall inside the Jersey City page. Hoboken has one ZIP, 07030, and its own page for the Hoboken emergency plumber line.

Farther out.

Newark is outside our regular zone. Call (718) 412-8453 and ask the dispatcher about a job there, and they will say plainly whether we can reach you. Staten Island is across the water and is not on our route.

What to have ready when you call.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Whether the problem is in a house, an apartment or a shop.
  • Where the main water shutoff is, if you know.
  • What you see, hear and smell, in plain words.

Dispatch uses that to pick the next available plumber and to bring the right parts. A short, specific description saves time on site.

For services, see the drain cleaning and water heater repair pages, which are the calls we take most often.

Outside these towns?

Call and ask. The dispatcher tells you straight whether we can reach your address.
