What Uptown means in Bayonne.

A Bayonne real estate guide describes Uptown as a family-oriented residential area with tree-lined streets and well-kept multi-family homes. NeighborhoodScout, a third-party site, uses the name North Bayonne and reports that about 55 percent of housing there is in two to four unit buildings.

The City of Bayonne history page says one and two family homes, small apartment buildings and small businesses predominate across the city. That fits what the guides describe for the north end.

Bayonne ZIP code 07002 covers the whole city. For the rest of the city see plumbing in Bayonne.

Sewer backups and basements.

A city notice from 2023 places the site of the 63rd Street Pumping Station in the 100-year floodplain. Bayonne's numbered streets run up toward the 60s at the north end, so the notice is a reminder that low ground exists in the north of the city too.

That is general context and not a prediction for your street. If a basement floor drain bubbles or a toilet gurgles when the washer drains, the main sewer line may be involved. See sewer line cleaning and sewer camera inspection.

Floor drain backup.

A drain that bubbles or overflows during heavy rain may need a sump pump check or a backwater valve review.

Slow drains in several fixtures.

When tub, sink and toilet all slow down together, the problem is usually in the main line and not at one fixture.

Water heater failure.

A tank that leaks or a heater that stops needs a quick decision. See water heater replacement.

Damp basement walls.

Moisture is not always a plumbing leak. See basement waterproofing and leak detection.

Multi-family homes: who handles what.

In a two or three family home, the owner usually controls the shared pieces, such as the main shutoff, the water heater and the main drain. Tenants control their own fixtures. Knowing which is which speeds up a call.

  • Find the main water shutoff and label it for everyone in the building..
  • Find the cleanout for the main drain if there is one, and keep the path clear..
  • Keep the owner's or manager's phone number where tenants can see it..

Work in Bayonne follows N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, the state plumbing subcode, which adopts the IAPMO National Standard Plumbing Code with New Jersey amendments. Permits go through the building department.

A permit is the owner's concern as much as the plumber's, so ask what your job needs before work starts.
