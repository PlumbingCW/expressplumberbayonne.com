Where Bergen Point is.

The City of Bayonne history page lists Bergen Point as one of five villages that joined to form the township in 1861. It is a section of Bayonne, not a separate town, and it uses Bayonne ZIP code 07002.

Collins Park runs along the Kill Van Kull at the southern end of the city. It covers about 40 acres, and the Bayonne Bridge approach crosses over its western end. The Hackensack RiverWalk starts at the park.

Older multi-unit buildings and what they mean for a call.

A third-party housing profile from NeighborhoodScout describes Bergen Point as having a large share of two to four unit buildings and a high share of homes built before 1940. Treat that as an estimate, since it is not a city record.

A building year does not tell us what is inside the walls. Owners replace pipes in pieces over the decades, so we look at what is actually there and do not guess a material from a date.

Two to four unit buildings.

A clog that shows up in more than one unit often points to a shared line. See drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

Low basements near the water.

Floor drains and sump pits matter most in low ground. See sump pump repair and basement waterproofing.

Hidden leaks and high bills.

A water meter that keeps moving with every fixture off suggests a leak. Leak detection finds it before walls are opened.

General guidance for low ground and flooding.

Bayonne sits at an elevation of about 7 feet, according to Wikipedia, and lower floors take water first after heavy rain. That is a general fact about the city and not a statement about any one Bergen Point street.

The city posted a notice on June 18, 2026 about a flood mitigation project near Cottage Street, East 5th Street and Ingham Avenue. It includes about 330 feet of new 36-inch relief sewer. The notice does not call it a Bergen Point project, so we cite it only as an example of work under way.

If a drain backs up.

  1. Stop running water in the unit, including toilets and the washer.
  2. Keep people and pets away from standing water, since it may carry sewage.
  3. Do not pour chemical cleaners into a backed-up line.
  4. Call us and describe which fixtures are affected.

Utilities, permits and licensing.

Veolia operates the water and sewer system in Bayonne, and the city keeps ownership. Veolia customer service is 888-434-0518, and a problem in the street starts with them.

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed master plumber or under one. The Bayonne building department is at 630 Avenue C, and it expects a permit to be filed within 72 hours after emergency work.
