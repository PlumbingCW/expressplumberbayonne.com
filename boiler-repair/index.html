No heat: what to check and when to call.

A boiler heats water or makes steam and sends it through pipes to radiators or baseboards. When it stops, the cause is usually fuel, ignition, water, controls or circulation. A plumber checks each in order and finds the failed part.

Call the emergency line when there is no heat in cold weather, when water is leaking from the boiler or its pipes, when the pressure gauge is far outside normal or when the boiler makes loud banging noises. Never ignore a gas smell or a carbon monoxide alarm. Leave first and call 911.

No heat at all.

Check the thermostat, the power switch and the gas valve. A tripped safety control or a failed ignition can also stop the burner.

Leaking boiler.

Water at the base can come from a relief valve, a pump seal or a failed tank.

Banging or kettling.

Mineral deposits or trapped air make noise as water heats.

Pressure too high or low.

A faulty expansion tank, feed valve or leak changes the pressure.

According to the master plumber rule, boiler repair involves gas, water and venting, so licensed work matters. The master plumber rule applies to the plumbing side, and PSE&G tells anyone who smells gas to leave, get 350 feet away and call 1-800-880-7734 or 911.

Older basement boilers in Bayonne.

Many older buildings heat with a basement boiler connected to cast iron radiators. Those systems last for decades but have leaks at valves, tired circulators and old expansion tanks. A plumber who knows older systems will check each of those.

PSE&G is replacing aging cast iron gas pipe in Bayonne, with work listed along Avenue C from 21st through 53rd Street. If your gas service or meter was reconnected, tell the plumber, so the boiler's gas supply and safety controls can be checked.

In a multi-family building, a boiler failure affects every unit. Tell the building owner or manager right away. The owner is usually responsible for the boiler in a rental, and a record of when you called protects you.

Insurance note.

Heating system failure from wear is usually not covered. A sudden leak that damages a room may be. Photograph the damage and the boiler label.

What to do while you wait for the plumber.

  • Check the thermostat is set to heat and the batteries are fresh..
  • Make sure the boiler switch is on and the breaker has not tripped..
