How a water heater installation works.

A water heater installation connects a new tank to cold and hot water lines, a gas or electric supply, a temperature and pressure relief valve and, for gas units, a vent that carries exhaust outside. A correct install also adds shutoff valves, a drain line for the relief valve and often an expansion tank.

Most installs take part of a day. The plumber drains and removes the old unit, sets the new one on a stable base, makes the connections, fires or energizes it, and checks for leaks and draft before leaving. Gas lines are pressure tested, and venting is checked with the unit running.

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees, according to the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. Installs follow the state plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and often need a permit.

We install in Hoboken condos and rowhouses too, where closet space and venting decide the model.

Choosing size, fuel and venting.

Size follows demand. A household that showers in the morning and runs a dishwasher and washer together needs more capacity than one person living alone. Fuel follows what the building has. A gas heater needs a gas line and a vent path that works, while an electric heater needs the right circuit.

Gas water heater venting needs special care. A vent that slopes the wrong way, leaks at a joint or shares a flue with an unsuitable appliance can let exhaust back into the room. If an existing gas heater shows signs of poor venting, see water heater repair before it becomes a safety problem.

Installing in Bayonne basements and two-family homes.

In a two-family house or a building with a shared basement, the heater can serve one unit or both. That affects tank size, shutoff placement and how you label each line. The plumber should confirm which unit each valve controls before work begins.

Bergen Point and Constable Hook both sit on the water. If a heater sits low in a basement that has taken water before, raising it on a stand keeps the burner and controls above minor flooding.

Ask the Bayonne building department at 630 Avenue C what permit applies to your install. Rules vary by project, and a plumber who does this work regularly will know what is typically filed.

Insurance note.

A planned install is not an insurance event. Check whether your policy covers water damage from a failed heater, and keep records of the install date and model.
