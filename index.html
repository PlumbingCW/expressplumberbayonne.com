Emergency plumber in Bayonne, NJ: what we handle at any hour.

An emergency plumber is a licensed plumber who answers the phone and dispatches around the clock, so a flooding basement at 3 a.m. gets the same response as a dripping faucet at noon. Bayonne Express Plumbing works from 569 Broadway and covers Bayonne first, then Jersey City and Hoboken.

Most emergency calls fall into a short list. One is a burst or leaking pipe. Others are a clogged drain or sewer backup, a failed water heater and a toilet that will not stop running. The last is a leak that shows up as a wet ceiling or a water meter that keeps spinning.

We answer the call, ask what you see and hear, and book the next available plumber. The plumber confirms the problem on site, explains the repair options, and gives you a clear price before work starts.

Which plumbing problems are real emergencies.

Some problems can wait until morning and some cannot. Anything that is damaging the building, putting wastewater in living space, or leaving you without heat or hot water in winter belongs on the emergency line.

Water you cannot stop.

A split supply pipe, a failed fitting or a flooding washing machine line. Close the main valve, then call about emergency burst pipe repair.

Sewage coming back up.

Toilets bubbling, floor drains backing up or several fixtures clogging at once points to the main line. See emergency sewer line cleaning.

No hot water.

A cold shower in July is an inconvenience. In January it is a problem for the whole household. We handle emergency water heater repair and replacement.

A leak you cannot find.

A wet ceiling, a warm patch on the floor or a meter that moves when every tap is off. Emergency leak detection finds the source before more wall opens up.

If you smell gas, do not call a plumber first. Leave the building, move away and call PSE&G at 1-800-880-7734 or 911 from outside. We can help with the plumbing side once the utility says the building is safe.

Bayonne water, sewers and weather: what they mean for your pipes.

Bayonne is a peninsula, with Newark Bay on the west and the Kill Van Kull on the south. Water and sewer service is operated by Veolia under a partnership with the city, which keeps ownership of the system. If a problem starts at the street or the water main, that is Veolia and the city. If it starts inside your walls or on your side of the meter, that is a plumber.

In January 2026 a damaged 30-inch main in South Kearny cut pressure across the city. Bayonne issued a boil water advisory from January 22 to January 24. After the advisory lifted, the city suggested running faucets for three to five minutes and draining and refilling water heaters set below 113 degrees Fahrenheit. If you saw cloudy water, new drips or a heater acting differently after that week, it is worth a check. See our notes on water heater problems.

Flooding is a local concern too. The city has posted public notices for a relief sewer and underground storage near Cottage Street Park, and for backup power at the 63rd Street Pumping Station, which the city's 2023 notice places in the 100-year floodplain on the preliminary flood map. If your basement is near the water, a working sump pump and an accessible floor drain matter more than they would inland.

Your first ten minutes during a plumbing emergency.

  1. Find the main shutoff valve, usually where the water line enters the basement near the meter, and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. If water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, stay clear and switch off power only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.
  3. Open a cold tap on the lowest floor to drain the lines, and move valuables, rugs and boxes out of the wet area.
  4. Take photos and short video of the damage before cleanup. Landlords and insurers ask for them.
  5. Call (718) 412-8453 and describe what you see. Tell the dispatcher about any gas smell, sewage or electrical contact.

If you rent, tell your landlord or property manager at the same time. Building shutoffs in multi-family houses often sit in a basement you cannot reach.
