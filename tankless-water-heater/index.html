What a tankless water heater does and what it needs.

A tankless unit turns on when you open a hot tap, fires a burner or energizes elements, and heats the water through a heat exchanger. It saves space and avoids standby heat loss. In exchange, it needs a gas line sized for a high burst of demand, correct venting and clean water flowing through the exchanger.

Many problems trace to one of three things: scale on the heat exchanger, a gas supply that is too small, or a vent that is blocked or poorly installed. Error codes help the plumber find the cause quickly.

A tankless install changes gas, vent or electric work, so it is covered by the state plumbing subcode and usually by permit. According to PSE&G guidance, you should leave and call 1-800-880-7734 or 911 if you smell gas, and that applies to any gas appliance.

Flush and descale: the maintenance most units need.

Hard water leaves mineral scale inside the heat exchanger. Scale narrows the passages, raises temperatures and triggers error codes. A flush and descale pushes a mild acid solution through the exchanger using the isolation valves, then rinses it clean.

How often a unit needs it depends on your water. Ask Veolia for its annual water quality report, which lists hardness and other results for the Bayonne system. Until you have the figure, a yearly check is a reasonable starting schedule.

Does a tankless water heater leak?

It can. Leaks usually come from isolation valves, loose fittings, a cracked heat exchanger or condensate lines on high-efficiency units. A leak under the unit does not always mean the exchanger has failed, so the plumber traces the water to its source first.

Leak location.Common cause.Fix.
Isolation valves.Worn packing or loose nut.Tighten or replace valve.
Bottom of the unit.Heat exchanger leak.Repair or replace unit.
Condensate line.Blocked or disconnected drain.Clear and reconnect.
Relief valve.Pressure spike or faulty valve.Test and replace valve.

Tankless units in Bayonne homes.

Switching from a tank to tankless often means a larger gas line and a new vent through the wall or roof. In a row house or multi-family building, finding a legal vent path can decide whether tankless is practical at all.

During the January 2026 boil water advisory, the city suggested that people with softeners and filters run them through a regeneration cycle once the advisory lifted. If you have a softener upstream of a tankless unit, it also protects the exchanger from scale.
