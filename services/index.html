How to choose the right service.

Plumbing problems overlap. A gurgling toilet can be a toilet issue, a drain issue or a sewer issue. The plumber confirms the cause on site, so a wrong guess on the phone costs you nothing. Call (718) 412-8453 and describe the symptom.

What you notice.Start with.Why.
Several drains slow or backing up.Sewer line cleaning.The main line serves every fixture.
One sink or tub drains slowly.Drain cleaning.A local clog is the likeliest cause.
Water bill jumped, no visible leak.Leak detection.Hidden leaks show up in the meter first.
Cold water only.Water heater repair.Thermostat, element, burner or valve faults.
Wet basement after rain.Sump pump repair.A failed pump or a drainage problem.
Old pipes failing repeatedly.Repiping.Replacing the run ends repeat repairs.

We also install new fixtures and equipment. See plumbing installation for sinks, toilets, filtration and gas appliance connections, and commercial plumbing for shops, restaurants and offices.

Seasonal problems follow a pattern. Freezing weather brings split pipes and failed water heaters. Heavy rain brings backups and wet basements. Summer brings running toilets and high water bills. A call early in a problem usually costs less than a call after the damage spreads.

Plumbing is regulated in New Jersey. The state says plumbing work is to be done by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees. Ask any plumber for a license number and check it with the Division of Consumer Affairs.
