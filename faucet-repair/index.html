Shower, tub and bathtub faucet repair.

A tub or shower valve that drips or will not shut off usually needs a new cartridge. The plumber shuts off the water, removes the trim and handle, replaces the cartridge and checks the valve seat. When the valve body is corroded or the access is poor, the repair is larger.
