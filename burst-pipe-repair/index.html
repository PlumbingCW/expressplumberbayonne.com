When a burst pipe is an emergency.

A burst pipe is an emergency every time, because water under pressure keeps flowing until someone shuts it off. A cracked, split or disconnected pipe sprays or pours, soaks insulation and drywall, and can reach outlets, appliances and the electrical panel.

Close the main valve first, usually in the basement where the line enters near the meter. Open a cold tap on the lowest floor to drain the lines. If water is near the electrical panel or outlets, stay out and call us and your electric utility before touching switches.

Frozen and split.

Water expands as it freezes, and the pipe splits where the ice formed. The leak starts when it thaws.

Failed fittings.

Old solder joints, compression fittings and push-fit connectors can let go under pressure.

Corroded pipe.

Galvanized and thin copper pipe can pinhole or burst as the wall thins.

Water hammer damage.

Repeated pressure spikes can crack fittings and weak sections.

According to the Bayonne building department's published page, contractors have 72 hours to come in for permits after emergency work. Repairs follow the state plumbing subcode, and the master plumber rule says licensed plumbers do the work.

Pipe leak repair: cracked, corroded or pinhole.

Burst pipe repair removes the damaged section and replaces it with new pipe and fittings. Copper is cut out and soldered or joined with press fittings. PEX is cut and connected with crimp or expansion fittings. Galvanized steel is cut out and replaced, and sometimes the plumber advises replacing more of the run.

Pipe material.Common failure.Typical repair.
Copper.Frozen split, pinhole, failed solder joint.Cut out section, new copper or press fitting.
PEX.Fitting leak, kinked or frozen split.Cut and replace with new fittings.
Galvanized steel.Corrosion, thin walls, pinholes.Replace section, consider more.
Cast iron drain.Crack, joint failure.Replace section or line.
Drain line in wall.Crack or loose joint.Camera first, then repair.

If several leaks show up in a short time, the pipe is wearing out, and spot repair may only delay the next failure. That is when repiping is worth pricing. Leaks that you cannot see are the job of leak detection.

Winter, old pipes and Bayonne basements.

Bayonne winters can stay below 32 degrees Fahrenheit for days, and a pipe in an unheated garage, a crawl space or an exterior wall is at risk once the cold lasts for hours.

Pipes that run close to a basement window, a rear porch or an uninsulated corner freeze first. Leave a cold tap dripping, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, and know where your main valve is before the cold arrives.

Veolia operates the water mains in Bayonne and the city owns the system. A break in the street main is theirs. A break in your service line or inside the building is a plumber's job. The meter test in our leak detection guide helps you tell which side you are on.

Insurance note.

Sudden, accidental pipe bursts are often covered for the water damage, though not usually for the pipe itself or for freezing from neglect. Photograph everything before cleanup and call your insurer promptly.
